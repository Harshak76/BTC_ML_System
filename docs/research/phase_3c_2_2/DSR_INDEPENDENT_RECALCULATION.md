## 3. Mathematical Foundations & Annualization Mechanics

To guarantee dimensional purity, the auditor documents exactly how annualization is applied to every term in the DSR equation:

### Where Annualization Is Applied:
1. **Observed Sharpe Ratio:**
   - Periodic: $SR_{\text{per}} = \frac{\mu_r}{\sigma_r}$
   - Annualized: $SR_{\text{ann}} = SR_{\text{per}} \cdot \sqrt{A}$, where $A = 8,760$
2. **Expected Maximum Sharpe Ratio ($SR^*$):**
   - Under extreme value theory (Euler-Mascheroni approximation):
     $$E[\max_N \{SR\}] \approx \sigma_{\text{trials}} \left( (1 - \gamma) \Phi^{-1}\left(1 - \frac{1}{N}\right) + \gamma \Phi^{-1}\left(1 - \frac{1}{N \cdot e}\right) \right)$$
   - Periodic Expected Max: Evaluated using $\sigma_{\text{per}} = 0.00629845 \implies SR^*_{\text{per}} = \mathbf{+0.01023525}$
   - Annualized Expected Max: $SR^*_{\text{ann}} = SR^*_{\text{per}} \cdot \sqrt{A} = 0.01023525 \cdot 93.59487 = \mathbf{+0.95796695}$
3. **Mertens Standard Error ($\sigma_{\widehat{SR}}$):**
   - The asymptotic variance of the Sharpe estimator under non-normality (Mertens, 2002):
     $$\sigma_{\widehat{SR}_{\text{per}}}^2 = \frac{1}{n-1} \left[ 1 - \gamma_3 \cdot SR_{\text{per}} + \frac{\gamma_4 - 1}{4} \cdot SR_{\text{per}}^2 \right]$$
   - Periodic SE: $\sigma_{\text{per}} = \sqrt{\sigma_{\widehat{SR}_{\text{per}}}^2}$
   - Annualized SE: $\sigma_{\text{ann}} = \sigma_{\text{per}} \cdot \sqrt{A}$
