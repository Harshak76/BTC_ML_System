### 2. Experimental Objectives & Hypotheses

#### 2.1 Core Research Question
Does conditioning 1-hour Donchian breakout signals on directional trend confirmation significantly improve out-of-sample risk-adjusted returns by filtering out false breakout whipsaws in consolidation regimes?

#### 2.2 Formal Null and Alternative Hypotheses
- **$H_0$ (Null Hypothesis):** Conditioning Donchian 40 breakout entries on $\text{ADX} \ge 25$ or 4-hour HTF trend alignment produces no statistically significant improvement in out-of-sample Sharpe ratio relative to the unfiltered baseline ($\Delta \text{Sharpe}_{\text{OOS}} \le 0$).
- **$H_1$ (Alternative Hypothesis A):** Gating on $\text{ADX} \ge 25$ significantly reduces trade count in neutral regimes, reducing drawdown and yielding $\Delta \text{Sharpe}_{\text{OOS}} > 0.50$.
- **$H_2$ (Alternative Hypothesis B):** Gating on Higher-Timeframe (4-hour) EMA 20/50 alignment eliminates counter-trend breakouts during higher-timeframe consolidations, yielding $\Delta \text{Sharpe}_{\text{OOS}} > 0.50$.

### 4. Technical Execution Assumptions & Friction Model

To ensure scientific comparability, all three arms will execute under the frozen Phase 3 execution assumptions:

1. **Signal Timing & Candle Completion:**
   - Signals are calculated strictly at the close of bar $t$ using completed candles (`enforce_completed_candles = True`).
   - Orders are queued for execution on bar $t+1$ open (`execution_timing = "next_bar_open"`).
   - Zero lookahead: indicators (Donchian, ADX, HTF EMA) use bar $t$ close or earlier.
2. **Execution Frictions & Transaction Costs:**
   - **US Equities:** 2 bps slippage on entry, 2 bps slippage on exit, 0 bps commission.
   - **Cryptocurrencies:** 5 bps slippage on entry, 5 bps slippage on exit, 10 bps commission.
   - Market session enforcement: Equity orders only fill during US market hours (08:00 to 23:00 UTC).
3. **Position Sizing & Risk Management:**
   - Fixed capital: $E_0 = \$100,000.00$ USD.
   - Risk per trade: 0.5% of total current equity.
   - Maximum concurrent open positions: 4.
   - Maximum capital per position: 25% of current equity.
4. **Stops & Target Anchoring:**
   - Initial Stop Loss: Fill price minus $2 \times \text{ATR}_{14}$ (anchored to execution fill price, not signal close).
   - Profit Target: Fill price plus $4 \times \text{ATR}_{14}$ (1:2 risk-to-reward ratio).
   - Trailing Stop: 2.0% trailing from highest high reached after entry.
   - Intrabar Collision Rule: If both stop-loss and take-profit are touched in the same bar, the stop-loss is filled (conservative assumption).
